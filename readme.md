I port ColorOS and OxygenOS 16 onto Snapdragon 888 hardware, write the kernels that keep
them alive, and build the interfaces that make them worth looking at.

Also: just a random Sabrina Carpenter fan who happens to write kernels. **Feather**,
**Espresso**, **Singular**, **Tornado** — every project I ship ends up named after one of
her songs, and at this point I've stopped pretending that's a coincidence.

### What I'm building

| Project | What it is | Stack |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **[Exhale](https://github.com/ozyern/Exhale)** | A breath of fresh air for your music. Open-source Android player built around a fluid Liquid Glass interface and a library scanner that's actually fast. | `Kotlin` |
| **[BrinaOS](https://github.com/ozyern/BrinaOS)** | Bash porting framework for ColorOS / OxygenOS 16 on SM8350 — partition work, smali patching, OTA generation, SuperVOOC paths, feature unlocking. | `Shell` |
| **[Feather Kernel](https://github.com/ozyern/Feather-Kernel-SM8350)** | Custom kernel for the OnePlus 9 / 9 Pro. Linux 5.4.302 with SukiSU Ultra and SusFS 2.2.0, packaged with AnyKernel3. | `C` |
| **[sabrina.ozyern.me](https://github.com/ozyern/sabrinacarpenter)** | My love letter to Sabrina Carpenter as a website — era-filtered discography, Dynamic Island nav, gallery, birthday campaign. | `HTML/CSS/JS` |
| **[ReVork](https://github.com/ozyern/ReVork)** | ~370-member Telegram community for custom ROM development. Themed roles, release channels, and people who actually answer questions. | `Community` |
| **[HyperOS_lemonadep](https://github.com/ozyern/HyperOS_lemonadep)** | Automatic HyperOS porter for the OnePlus 9 Pro. | `Python` |

Plus a small pile of KernelSU/Magisk spoof modules —
[OnePlus 15](https://github.com/ozyern/oneplus15-spoofer),
[Find X9 Ultra](https://github.com/ozyern/FindX9Ultra-spoofer),
[S26 Ultra](https://github.com/ozyern/s26-ultra-spoofer) — for when a device needs to
believe it's something else for a minute.
